## Executive Summary
Based on qualitative analysis of X reviews, describe the findings and confidence in those findings in 300 words or less.

## Data Profile
Build a table describing the reviews processed with the product distributions relative to the overall dataset

## Market Profile
Describe the market in terms of the needs-based segments observed from the reviews.
Describe how the clustering arrived at the optimal number of segments.

For each segment, add the following details:
- Who we think they are (inferred firmographics)
- What they are saying they need
- Complaint patterns
- Sentiment toward each product

### Per-Attribute ProductXSegment Fit 
For each attribute, build a table of the products as rows and segments as columns. The cells should be filled with perceptual distance from the segments centroid

### Per-Segment Centroid
Based on the combination of product attribute data outside the corpus, and directional sentiment within the corpus, what is the directional inferred set of "ideal" attribute values for each segment (where could a conjoint study add value as a next step)
